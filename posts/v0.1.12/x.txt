POST 2 - a reply to post 1

Also in v0.1.12: nothing we ship has an executable stack any more.

RandomX ships assembly with no stack note, so the linker marked the whole
node RWE. Found with readelf by somebody who had to build Core from source
to get around it.

NOTES

No block height and no hash rate. Both move, an X post does not, and a stale
number is worse than no number on the one platform where a post outlives its
own facts.

The word MANDATORY is not used here. It is in the release notes and in the
Discord and Telegram notices the bot sends, where it is one click from the
explanation. On a timeline it would arrive without that, and a warning that
cannot be read in full is a warning spent.
